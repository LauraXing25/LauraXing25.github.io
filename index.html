ROBOTICS · SENSING · HUMAN–ROBOT INTERACTION

Hello, I'm Laura ✳

I build sensing systems that help robots perceive physical contact and interact more safely and intelligently with the world.

I am an M.S. student in Mechanical Engineering at Columbia University, conducting research in the Robotic Manipulation and Mobility (ROAM) Lab with Prof. Matei Ciocarlie. My work focuses on large-area robotic skin, tactile sensing, embedded electronics, and intelligent sensing systems.

Previously, I received my B.S. in Biomedical Engineering from Johns Hopkins University, where I worked on distributed tactile sensing and compressed sensing approaches to scalable artificial skin.

LEAPS / PVDF SKIN
COLUMBIA UNIVERSITY · 2025–PRESENT

LEAPS: Large-Area Dynamic Robotic Skin

Developing flexible PVDF-based tactile skin with low-noise readout electronics and high-rate sensing to detect transient contact across robot surfaces. Exploring robust sensing, sensitivity tradeoffs, and responsive robot safety behaviors.

SPTS / TACTILE ARRAY
JOHNS HOPKINS UNIVERSITY · 2023–2025

Scalable Tactile Sensing via Compressed Sensing

Designed and investigated a distributed flexible tactile array and compressed sensing framework to reduce sampling demands while preserving contact localization. The project combines sensor hardware, embedded communication, and sparse reconstruction.
